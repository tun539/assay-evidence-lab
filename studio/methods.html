Evidence workspace

The portable Evidence Workspace stores structured records under schema assay-evidence/1 in browser localStorage. Each record carries an evidence kind (measured or simulated), dataset name, method, metric, finite numeric value, unit, provenance URL, timestamp, and a JSON details object.

Direct metric subtraction is enabled only when two records share the same evidence type, dataset, metric, unit, and comparisonKey. Records with different scopes are displayed side by side without computing a misleading delta. Packets export to JSON (lossless re-import), CSV (tabular summary), and Markdown (peer-review report).

Enzyme kinetics & OOD benchmark workbench

The primary benchmark uses 23 initial-velocity measurements from M. A. Treloar’s galactosyltransferase study (distributed with R’s Puromycin dataset), alongside four controlled synthetic mechanistic benchmarks (non-competitive pure-Vmax shift, competitive pure-Km cliff, allosteric positive cooperativity with Hill n ≈ 2.2, and Haldane uncompetitive substrate inhibition with high-dose dead-end ESS cliff) plus custom browser CSV upload.

Six positive-parameter hypotheses—Shared Vmax & Km (k=2), Condition Vmax (k=3), Condition Km (k=3), Condition Vmax & Km (k=4), Cooperative Hill (k=6), and Haldane Substrate Inhibition v = Vmax·[S]/(Km + [S] + [S]²/Ki) (k=6)—are fitted by exact profile nonlinear least squares in studio/kinetics-analytics.js (verified to <0.002 counts/min/min against SciPy least_squares). Inspired by out-of-distribution and cliff evaluation practices from molML (TU Eindhoven), each leave-one-concentration-out fold is classified into In-Domain Interpolation (interior concentrations) vs. OOD Boundary Extrapolation (lowest and highest [S]) and compared against the irreducible pure replicate noise floor spure, small-sample AICc, Akaike weights, Lin’s Concordance Correlation Coefficient (CCC), exact F0.95 2D joint confidence contours, and Fisher-information active-learning sensitivity curves.

Molecular learning lab

Synthetic enzyme curves are generated from v = Vmax · S / (Km + S) with additive Gaussian noise across six substrate concentrations. Four parameter estimators (nonlinear Michaelis–Menten fit, ridge regression, scikit-learn random forest, and a two-hidden-layer PyTorch MLP) predict Vmax and Km from all six rates. Separately, a tabular Q-learning policy observes an initial rate at 0.11 ppm, selects a second concentration, and evaluates a fitted Gaussian decoder on held-out synthetic curves.

Cellpose instance segmentation & AlphaFold confidence audit workbench

Track A · Cellpose Kuhn–Munkres instance matching & Panoptic Quality

In studio/audit-engine.js (exact browser counterpart to evidence/audit.py and vendored MouseLand/cellpose/metrics.py at a54cb48), arbitrary sparse 2D integer label maps are normalized to 0..K, contingency pixel overlaps build the exact pairwise Intersection-over-Union matrix, and an exact O(N²M) Kuhn–Munkres / Jonker–Volgenant augmenting-path solver minimizes cost = −𝕀(IoU ≥ τ) − IoU / (2 N_min). Alongside canonical Cellpose match scores AP(τ) = TP / (TP + FP + FN) across τ ∈ [0.30, 0.95] and COCO mAP@[0.50:0.95:0.05], the workbench decomposes Kirillov et al. (2019) Panoptic Quality PQ = SQ × RQ, 1-px morphological contour Boundary F1, foreground Pixel Dice, and overlap-graph failure taxonomy (Under-segmented Merges, Over-segmented Splits, Boundary Shifts, Missed Dim FN, and Spurious Debris FP) across five segmentation architectures (Cellpose-cyto3, StarDist-2D, Distance Watershed, Semantic U-Net, and Mis-tuned Flow).

Track B · AlphaFold PredictedLDDTHead logits, calibration & PAE pTM

Matching vendored google-deepmind/alphafold/common/confidence.py (c77e5d2) to <10−12, temperature-scaled softmax converts raw residue-by-bin logits z / T into probabilities over bin centers c_b, yielding expected per-residue pLDDT, predictive bin spread ±σ_pLDDT, and normalized Shannon entropy Ĥ ∈ [0, 1] (separating uniform epistemic ignorance from bimodal hinge conformations). Across all 10 bundled KEGG/AlphaFold DB v6 monomers, flagship multi-entity PDB 8AW3, and the 4-residue classroom fixture, the workbench computes post-hoc temperature scaling Expected Calibration Error (ECE, Guo et al. 2017) and Jumper et al. (2021, Eq. 38) Lorentzian pTM global packing scores and rigid-domain blocks directly from the 48 × 48 PAE matrices, while preserving lossless import/export of evidence-audit/1 JSON reports.

Protein, nucleic acid, and complex 3D structures

The 3D Molecular & Gene Structure Studio combines 8 experimental RCSB PDB macromolecular structures and 10 KEGG-linked AlphaFold DB v6 gene-product predictions (18 bundled 3D models total), plus local user .pdb file parsing directly in the browser.

Experimental PDB structures:

  • PDB 8AW3 — Multi-entity Protein–RNA–2×Zn²⁺ complex (AlphaFold Server reference example: protein chains 2 and 3, 73-nt tRNA chain 1 with ribose C4′ + base tips, and 2 coordinated Zn²⁺ ions; published AlphaFold Server reference readouts ipTM = 0.78, pTM = 0.75).
  • PDB 1UBQ — Human ubiquitin (Vijay-Kumar, Bugg & Cook, 1.8 Å X-ray, 76 Cα).
  • PDB 1GFL — Aequorea victoria Green Fluorescent Protein β-barrel (230 Cα).
  • PDB 1MBO — Sperm whale Oxymyoglobin with covalent Heme porphyrin (HEM) and bound dioxygen (OXY) ligand coordinates (153 Cα + 45 ligand atoms).
  • PDB 1BNA — Dickerson–Drew B-DNA dodecamer experimental duplex (2 antiparallel chains × 12 nt with base tips).
  • Additional chain-A folds: Glucagon (1GCN) · Crambin (1CRN) · ST0493 (2RBG).

Geometry, secondary structure, and confidence: Straight-line distances compare Cα (or nucleic acid C4′) positions in ångströms; radius of gyration weights resolved backbone atoms equally, and maximum span is the largest pairwise Euclidean distance. The offline 3D cartoon renderer (studio/mol3d-engine.js) classifies α-helices and β-strands from local Cα geometry and renders Catmull–Rom ribbons with Blinn–Phong shading. For the ten AlphaFold DB v6 gene predictions, per-residue pLDDT scores (0–100) come directly from the PDB B-factor column, and the 48×48 Expected Position Error (PAE, 0–30 Å) matrices are downsampled directly from each model’s official AlphaFold DB PAE JSON file.

DNA and gene records (10 linked genes)

The strand challenge uses the first 60 nucleotides of each oriented KEGG coding sequence; the AlphaFold 3 / Mol*-style 3D B-DNA workbench loads the complete coding DNA sequence (318–1,416 bp) using right-handed B-DNA geometry (3.38 Å axial rise, 10.5 bp/turn, asymmetric 22 Å major and 12 Å minor grooves), Catmull–Rom sugar–phosphate ribbons, deoxyribose pentoses, fused 5+6 purine and 6-ring pyrimidine planar slabs, all-atom CPK ball-and-stick mode, VdW groove spacefill, and 2 (A=T) or 3 (G≡C) dashed Watson–Crick hydrogen bonds. Any selected codon translates directly to its residue index in the paired AlphaFold DB v6 3D protein fold (using the vertebrate mitochondrial table for MT-ATP6 and the standard genetic code for the nine nuclear/bacterial genes).

Gene expression atlas

The Golub leukemia microarray benchmark contains 38 training specimens and 34 test specimens. Numeric sample IDs join each expression column to its diagnosis. The atlas reports class means in each cohort. Each model uses 25 ANOVA-selected training probes and training-derived scaling. The number of probes includes controls and repeated gene targets.

Cytology classifier

NucleusAudit uses 569 Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer specimens. The primary pipeline fits standardized logistic regression using 455 training specimens and evaluates predictions on a stratified holdout of 114. The nested cross-validation benchmark compares logistic regression, k-nearest neighbors, RBF SVM, random forest, and a class-prior baseline on 5 × 4 stratified folds with paired bootstrap Brier intervals.

Cryo-ET & computational tomography workbench (“Inside the specimen”)

Sixteen macromolecular and analytical specimens—ten KEGG-linked AlphaFold DB v6 monomers, four multi-entity RCSB PDB structures (8AW3, 1MBO, 1GFL, 1BNA with atomic-number electron scattering contrast), the 10-ellipsoid Modified Shepp–Logan phantom, and a 4-ellipsoid geometric reference—are sliced onto 64 × 64 or 96 × 96 grids and projected via a transpose-exact bilinear Radon operator (⟨Ax, y⟩ = ⟨x, A⊤y⟩) across 4–72 tilts spanning 180°, 120° (±60° Cryo-ET missing wedge), or 90° (±45°). Optional forward physics simulates 300 kV weak-phase Contrast Transfer Function oscillations CTF(k), phase-flipping restoration, seeded low-dose Poisson + readout noise (12–200 e−/Å2), and a 2× fine + 0.35 px shifted detector forward model to test Kaipio & Somersalo’s Inverse Crime.

Six canonical reconstruction algorithms are benchmarked simultaneously: simultaneous iterative WLS (SIRT), golden-stride ordered-angle SART, Kak–Slaney ramp Filtered Back-Projection (FBP Ram-Lak), Cryo-ET Hann-apodized WBP, multiplicative Poisson MLEM, and Chambolle–Nesterov isotropic Total Variation compressed sensing (CS-TV FISTA). Diagnostics report pixelwise RMSE, SSIM, PSNR, Lin’s CCC, radial Fourier Ring Correlation FRC(k) with the 0.143 gold-standard cutoff vs. Crowther’s πD/Nθ limit, reference-free Leave-One-Tilt-Out validation, Hansen’s L-curve, and a 120-run 10-gene Leave-One-Protein-Out benchmark separating projection-only sinogram learners from oracle reference-density neighbors.

3D single-particle tracking (SPT) & anomalous diffusion workbench

Ensembles of 64–144 3D tracers are simulated at 40 Hz (Δt = 0.025 s, 160 frames = 4.0 s) across eight generative stochastic regimes: isotropic Fickian Brownian diffusion (⟨r2⟩ = 6Dt), motor/advective drift (6Dt + v2t2), harmonic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck confinement (3D(1 − e−2kt)/k), anisotropic filament diffusion (Dx = D(1 + k)), pulsatile flow (v0 cos πt), viscoelastic fractional Brownian motion (AnDi fBm via exact 160 × 160 Toeplitz–Cholesky fractional Gaussian noise covariance γ(k) = DαΔtα(|k+1|α + |k−1|α − 2|k|α)), renewal continuous-time random walk transient traps (AnDi CTRW with Pareto waiting times ψ(τ) = ατ0ατ−1−α and exact Mittag-Leffler hop scaling), and Spot-On continuous-time 2-state Bound ⇌ Free chromatin switching (Hansen et al., eLife 2018). Macromolecular presets compute exact 3D radii of gyration Rg from the 10 bundled KEGG/AlphaFold DB v6 monomers and 4 RCSB PDB complexes and evaluate Stokes–Einstein diffusivity D = kBT / (6πηeffRh) (Rh = 1.29Rg, 310.15 K).

Live-cell camera observation adds static localization error σloc (0–80 nm) and exact Brownian-bridge shutter exposure motion blur R ∈ [0, 1/6]. Following Michalet & Berglund (Phys. Rev. E 2012) and Vestergaard et al. (2014), the Covariance-Based Estimator D̂_CVE = ⟨Δx_n²⟩/(2Δt) + ⟨Δx_n Δx_{n+1}⟩/Δt cancels both static localization noise and motion blur while simultaneously estimating σ̂loc alongside its Cramér–Rao Lower Bound (CRLB). Ten diagnostic panels audit ensemble vs. time-averaged MSD, weak ergodicity breaking EB(Δ), Van Hove kurtosis α2(t), velocity autocorrelation Cv(τ), 3D turning angles, Spot-On 3D Maxwell jump mixtures, optical-reciprocity Virtual 3D FRAP recovery, 3D absorbing-sphere first-passage survival S(t), and 7-candidate out-of-sample trajectory inference (nats/coord, AIC, and Akaike weights).

Molecular force-field, symplectic MD & conformational ensemble workbench

Track A compares five physical reference potentials (anharmonic quartic, Morse bond dissociation, Lennard–Jones 12–6, 3BPA-inspired dihedral torsion, and reactive double-well) against five analytical conservative surrogates (Harmonic Hooke, degree-4 Taylor polynomial, conservative gradient-kernel GP with exact epistemic σF, MACE-inspired Radial–Bessel + confining prior, and a 4-member tanh neural committee) integrated under Velocity Verlet, Forward Euler, RK4, or BAOAB Langevin dynamics. Following Fu et al. (ICLR 2023, “Forces Are Not Enough”), models are audited across OOD trajectory stability, Hamiltonian energy conservation ΔH(t), phase-space geometry, velocity-autocorrelation vibrational spectra S(ω), and active-learning DFT anchor acquisition. Canonical Quartic + Harmonic exports verify to <10−10 against vendored MACE metrics via python evidence/check_simulation.py.

Track B contrasts correlated BAOAB Langevin MD and Metropolis MCMC against Well-Tempered Metadynamics (with umbrella bias reweighting) and BioEmu-inspired uncorrelated equilibrium generation across discrete two-state switches, 1D cryptic-pocket barriers, 2D Müller–Brown surfaces, and 2D Alanine Dipeptide (φ, ψ) Ramachandran free-energy maps coupled to 3D Cα conformational breathing on PDB 1MBO, 1UBQ, and AlphaFold P04637.

Language and physics lab
