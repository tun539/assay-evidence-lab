Prediction audit

Cellpose · instance matching

The teaching example uses synthetic label maps. The evaluator computes the intersection over union of each object pair, assigns one-to-one matches and counts true positives, false positives and false negatives at each threshold. The displayed score is TP / (TP + FP + FN).

AlphaFold · residue confidence

For each residue, softmax converts synthetic confidence logits into probabilities over bins. The probability-weighted bin centers give the predicted confidence score (pLDDT) on a 0–100 scale. The mean summarizes the chain and can hide low-confidence residues.

Protein structures

Human ubiquitin uses 76 alpha-carbon coordinates from chain A of the experimental 1.8 Å X-ray structure. The viewer measures the straight-line distance between selected Cα atoms in ångströms. The default contact cutoff is 8 Å. The cutoff and minimum sequence separation are adjustable.

Vijay-Kumar, Bugg and Cook · PDB 1UBQ · Deposited coordinates

Additional experimental chain-A traces: Glucagon (1GCN) · Crambin (1CRN) · ST0493 (2RBG). Radius of gyration weights each resolved Cα equally. Maximum span is the largest pairwise Euclidean distance.

The five gene products use chain-A coordinates from AlphaFold DB version 6. A distance measured in one of these models describes the predicted fold. The gene and model table links to the exact model files used.

Enzyme kinetics

The 23 Puromycin measurements come from M. A. Treloar’s study of galactosyltransferase, distributed with R. Four Michaelis–Menten models share or separate the two kinetic parameters by condition. Each validation fold holds out one substrate concentration across both conditions and the reported RMSE pools the held-out rate errors.

Gene expression atlas

The Golub leukemia microarray benchmark contains 38 training specimens and 34 test specimens. Numeric sample IDs join each expression column to its diagnosis. The atlas reports class means in each cohort. Each model uses 25 ANOVA-selected training probes and training-derived scaling. The number of probes includes controls and repeated gene targets.

Cytology classifier

NucleusAudit uses 569 Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer specimens. The pipeline fits standardized logistic regression using 455 training specimens and evaluates predictions on a stratified holdout of 114. Features summarize nuclei in digitized aspirates.

Molecular transport in 3D

Each tracer starts at the origin. Free increments are independent Gaussian draws of variance 2DΔt per axis. Drift adds vΔt along x. For a harmonic trap each coordinate is multiplied by exp(−kΔt), then independent Gaussian noise of variance D(1−exp(−2kΔt))/k is added. Curves compare the mean squared displacement across 96 tracers with its analytical expectation. The finite-ensemble centroid and RMS distance are calculated from the same coordinates used for rendering.

Simulations

The force-law exercise fits a harmonic model to a synthetic one-dimensional potential. Its independent checker uses error metrics from MACE. The ensemble exercise uses a two-state Markov chain to explore finite sampling. BioEmu inspired the ensemble question. The trajectories shown here are generated by the classroom simulator.
