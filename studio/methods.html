Evidence workspace

The portable Evidence Workspace stores structured records under schema assay-evidence/1 in browser localStorage. Each record carries an evidence kind (measured or simulated), dataset name, method, metric, finite numeric value, unit, provenance URL, timestamp, and a JSON details object.

Direct metric subtraction is enabled only when two records share the same evidence type, dataset, metric, unit, and comparisonKey. Records with different scopes are displayed side by side without computing a misleading delta. Packets export to JSON (lossless re-import), CSV (tabular summary), and Markdown (peer-review report).

Enzyme kinetics

The 23 Puromycin measurements come from M. A. Treloar’s study of galactosyltransferase, distributed with R. Four Michaelis–Menten models share or separate the two apparent kinetic parameters by condition. Each validation fold holds out one substrate concentration across both conditions and the reported RMSE pools the held-out rate errors.

Molecular learning lab

Synthetic enzyme curves are generated from v = Vmax · S / (Km + S) with additive Gaussian noise across six substrate concentrations. Four parameter estimators (nonlinear Michaelis–Menten fit, ridge regression, scikit-learn random forest, and a two-hidden-layer PyTorch MLP) predict Vmax and Km from all six rates. Separately, a tabular Q-learning policy observes an initial rate at 0.11 ppm, selects a second concentration, and evaluates a fitted Gaussian decoder on held-out synthetic curves.

Prediction audit

Cellpose · instance matching

The teaching example uses synthetic label maps. The evaluator computes the intersection over union of each object pair, assigns one-to-one matches and counts true positives, false positives and false negatives at each threshold. The displayed score is TP / (TP + FP + FN).

AlphaFold · residue confidence

For each residue, softmax converts synthetic confidence logits into probabilities over bins. The probability-weighted bin centers give the predicted confidence score (pLDDT) on a 0–100 scale. The mean summarizes the chain and can hide low-confidence residues.

Protein, nucleic acid, and complex 3D structures

The 3D Molecular & Gene Structure Studio combines 8 experimental RCSB PDB macromolecular structures and 10 KEGG-linked AlphaFold DB v6 gene-product predictions (18 bundled 3D models total), plus local user .pdb file parsing directly in the browser.

Experimental PDB structures:

  • PDB 8AW3 — Multi-entity Protein–RNA–2×Zn²⁺ complex (AlphaFold Server reference example: protein chains 2 and 3, 73-nt tRNA chain 1 with ribose C4′ + base tips, and 2 coordinated Zn²⁺ ions; published AlphaFold Server reference readouts ipTM = 0.78, pTM = 0.75).
  • PDB 1UBQ — Human ubiquitin (Vijay-Kumar, Bugg & Cook, 1.8 Å X-ray, 76 Cα).
  • PDB 1GFL — Aequorea victoria Green Fluorescent Protein β-barrel (230 Cα).
  • PDB 1MBO — Sperm whale Oxymyoglobin with covalent Heme porphyrin (HEM) and bound dioxygen (OXY) ligand coordinates (153 Cα + 45 ligand atoms).
  • PDB 1BNA — Dickerson–Drew B-DNA dodecamer experimental duplex (2 antiparallel chains × 12 nt with base tips).
  • Additional chain-A folds: Glucagon (1GCN) · Crambin (1CRN) · ST0493 (2RBG).

Geometry, secondary structure, and confidence: Straight-line distances compare Cα (or nucleic acid C4′) positions in ångströms; radius of gyration weights resolved backbone atoms equally, and maximum span is the largest pairwise Euclidean distance. The offline 3D cartoon renderer (studio/mol3d-engine.js) classifies α-helices and β-strands from local Cα geometry and renders Catmull–Rom ribbons with Blinn–Phong shading. For the ten AlphaFold DB v6 gene predictions, per-residue pLDDT scores (0–100) come directly from the PDB B-factor column, and the 48×48 Expected Position Error (PAE, 0–30 Å) matrices are downsampled directly from each model’s official AlphaFold DB PAE JSON file.

DNA and gene records (10 linked genes)

The strand challenge uses the first 60 nucleotides of each oriented KEGG coding sequence; the AlphaFold 3 / Mol*-style 3D B-DNA workbench loads the complete coding DNA sequence (318–1,416 bp) using right-handed B-DNA geometry (3.38 Å axial rise, 10.5 bp/turn, asymmetric 22 Å major and 12 Å minor grooves), Catmull–Rom sugar–phosphate ribbons, deoxyribose pentoses, fused 5+6 purine and 6-ring pyrimidine planar slabs, all-atom CPK ball-and-stick mode, VdW groove spacefill, and 2 (A=T) or 3 (G≡C) dashed Watson–Crick hydrogen bonds. Any selected codon translates directly to its residue index in the paired AlphaFold DB v6 3D protein fold (using the vertebrate mitochondrial table for MT-ATP6 and the standard genetic code for the nine nuclear/bacterial genes).

Gene expression atlas

The Golub leukemia microarray benchmark contains 38 training specimens and 34 test specimens. Numeric sample IDs join each expression column to its diagnosis. The atlas reports class means in each cohort. Each model uses 25 ANOVA-selected training probes and training-derived scaling. The number of probes includes controls and repeated gene targets.

Cytology classifier

NucleusAudit uses 569 Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer specimens. The primary pipeline fits standardized logistic regression using 455 training specimens and evaluates predictions on a stratified holdout of 114. The nested cross-validation benchmark compares logistic regression, k-nearest neighbors, RBF SVM, random forest, and a class-prior baseline on 5 × 4 stratified folds with paired bootstrap Brier intervals.

Computational tomography

AlphaFold protein chain-A traces (or four synthetic ellipsoids for geometric calibration) define a 3D density field. Each view sums slice pixels into nearest detector bins. A simultaneous iterative solver backprojects detector residuals normalized by ray occupancy, averages across views and clips negative densities. Error is pixelwise RMSE against the known 64 × 64 slice. Export includes raw angle-major measurements, target and recovered pixels.

Molecular transport in 3D

Each tracer starts at the origin. Free increments are independent Gaussian draws of variance 2DΔt per axis. Drift adds vΔt along x. For a harmonic trap each coordinate is multiplied by exp(−kΔt), then independent Gaussian noise of variance D(1−exp(−2kΔt))/k is added. The directional model assigns x diffusivity D(1+k). Periodic flow integrates velocity v cos(πt) along x. The inference experiment fits conditional Gaussian increments to 64 complete trajectories and evaluates log density on 32 held-out trajectories. Curves compare the mean squared displacement across 96 tracers with its analytical expectation.

Language and physics lab

A bag-of-words multinomial linear classifier maps English and Spanish commands across 12 structured action–object–color meanings using disjoint training and test phrasing templates. Equivalent implementations in NumPy, PyTorch, and TensorFlow are provided in the companion notebook. An independent grammar validator prevents physics execution when a prompt is out of scope or disagrees with the predicted meaning.
