Scientific Provenance · MACE, MDsim, BioEmu & Independent Verification

1. MACE & Conservative Potentials

Inspired by MACE (Batatia et al., NeurIPS 2022), every surrogate here integrates an exact analytical energy Û(x) with F̂(x) = −∇Û(x). Exported canonical force reports verify to <10⁻¹⁰ against vendored MACE metrics via python evidence/check_simulation.py simulation-report.json.

2. “Forces Are Not Enough” (MDsim)

Following Fu et al. (ICLR 2023), static test-set force MAE can hide catastrophic polynomial OOD holes (Û → −∞), non-symplectic integrator drift, or distorted vibrational spectra S(ω) computed from the velocity autocorrelation Cvv(τ).

3. BioEmu & Free-Energy Landscapes

Motivated by Microsoft BioEmu (Lewis et al., 2025), short Langevin MD trajectories suffer Kramers exponential trapping (τ ∝ exp(ΔG‡/kBT)), whereas generative i.i.d. equilibrium emulators and Well-Tempered Metadynamics recover multi-basin ΔG across cryptic pockets and 2D (φ, ψ) space.
