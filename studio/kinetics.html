Data and assumptions

The 23 initial-rate measurements come from the R Puromycin dataset. The curves use v = Vmax × S / (Km + S). At the lowest and highest holdouts the curve must extrapolate beyond the training concentrations. These are apparent parameters fitted to the rate data.
