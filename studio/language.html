Can two languages drive the same scene? Follow a command from token counts to a structured action and test what changes when words are removed.

Use drop or slide, ball or cube, and red, blue or green. Spanish equivalents are suelta or desliza, esfera or cubo, and rojo, azul or verde.

Inspect the model
    Token positions show three centered coordinates of the learned class-weight vectors. Paths use all 12 dimensions and shortest paths on an undirected four-nearest-neighbor graph. Beams represent signed token contributions to the selected meaning’s logit.
    Class entropy measures uncertainty over the 12 authored meanings. An independent grammar check blocks physics when the input is unsupported or the predicted meaning disagrees with the parse.
    

    Reproduce the training
    Training and test prompts use disjoint templates while sharing the same meaning inventory. The notebook builds the dataset in Pandas and trains equivalent classifiers in NumPy, PyTorch and TensorFlow.
